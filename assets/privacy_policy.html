Welcome to KaSpot. We built this app around a simple principle: your location belongs to you. This Privacy Policy explains what information KaSpot collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language because you deserve to understand it, not just check a box.

✅ KaSpot never sells your data, never runs ads, and never shares your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

KaSpot collects only what is necessary for the app to work.

  • Account information — your email address and display name, provided when you create an account.
  • Profile picture — an optional photo you choose to upload. It is only shown to people in your circle.
  • Location data — your real-time GPS coordinates, collected only when you explicitly turn on location sharing. KaSpot keeps only your single most recent position. No history, no routes, no movement trails are ever stored.
  • Circle messages — short text messages exchanged with people in your circle through the in-app chat.
  • Circle connections — the mutual connections you establish with other KaSpot users (requires consent from both sides).
  • Notification preferences — your in-app settings for alerts and sharing behavior.
  • Device token — a unique identifier used to deliver push notifications to your device. This is generated by your device's operating system and is not linked to your identity outside of KaSpot.

How We Use Your Information

  • Location — shared only with circle members you have individually authorized. Removed from our servers immediately when you disable sharing.
  • Messages — delivered to the recipient and stored securely on our servers. Only you and the person you are chatting with can read your messages within the app.
  • Push notifications — sent to your device when a circle member messages you or sends a connection request, if you have notifications enabled.
  • Profile information — displayed to your circle members so they can identify you on the map and in the circle list.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond making the app work for you.

Zero-Trail Location Design

📍 KaSpot is designed around zero-trail privacy. Only your latest GPS coordinate is ever stored — one row in the database, overwritten with every update. The moment you turn off sharing, that row is permanently deleted. We have no record of where you have been.

Location data is only uploaded when sharing is active and only visible to circle members who have been individually granted access by you.

Who Can Access Your Data

  • You — full control over your profile, connections, and sharing settings at all times.
  • Your circle members — can see your name, profile picture, and location only if you have granted them sharing permission.
  • KaSpot administrators — as the operator of this service, we have administrative access to the database for the purposes of security monitoring, debugging, and ensuring the app functions correctly. This access is not used to read your messages for personal purposes.
  • Third parties — we do not share, sell, or transfer your personal data to any third party. Ever.

Data Storage and Security

All KaSpot data is stored on secure cloud servers protected by industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security policies — each user can only read data they are authorized to see.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

Data Retention

  • Location data — deleted immediately when you disable sharing.
  • Messages — retained while your account is active so your conversation history is available to you. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Account data — retained until you request account deletion.
  • Profile pictures — retained until you remove or replace them, or delete your account.

Push Notifications

KaSpot uses your device's built-in notification system to deliver alerts such as new messages and circle invitations. You can disable notifications at any time in the app under Account & Privacy → Notifications, or through your device system settings.

Notification delivery uses a secure token unique to your device. This token is not used to track you or shared with any advertising service.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access — you can view your profile, connections, and messages directly in the app at any time.
  • Correction — update your display name and profile picture at any time in Account & Privacy.
  • Deletion — you may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the address below. All data including location history, messages, and profile information will be permanently removed.
  • Location sharing control — you can pause or stop location sharing at any time directly from the map screen. No data is collected when sharing is off.
  • Notifications — you can disable push notifications at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

KaSpot is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you through the app before the changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this document will always reflect the most recent version.

Continuing to use KaSpot after a policy update means you accept the revised terms. If you disagree with the changes, you may delete your account at any time.
